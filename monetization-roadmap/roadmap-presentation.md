## Key Data Points (Existing Accounts)

- **24%** of accounts use AI credits (since release)
- **54%** opted-in users
- **32%** use any AI feature
- **3.6%** use ≥1K credits
- **40%** of using accounts used <100 credits
- **83%** of accounts used credits on AI columns and automations
- **45%** of credits used on Agents
- **72%** of accounts have only 1 user using AI credits
- **67%** of usage is by admin users
- **60%** of usage is by admin-only accounts

**Read top to bottom:** People built agents → at scale → that teams keep running → and they do real work.
