# PM Goals & Personal Roadmap

**Role:** Product Manager @ monday.com
**Focus:** AI-Augmented Independence & Strategic Leverage
**Updated:** February 2026

---

## 1. PRDs & market research (top priority)
- **Documentation speed:** Use AI to reduce time spent writing first-draft PRDs and User Stories by 70%, focusing time on refining logic rather than typing prose.
- **Automated specs:** Use AI (Claude/GPT) to generate deep technical specs from high-level PRDs, including API contracts, data schemas, and edge-case logic.
- **Market research:** Use AI to synthesize competitor analysis, market trends, and user research into actionable product insights.
- **Edge-case mastery:** Use AI to generate "What-If" scenarios to stress-test product logic before it reaches engineering.

## 2. Strategic leverage (the "how I work")
- **Zero-manual synthesis:** Automate 100% of qualitative feedback loops (Slack, Support, Gong) using AI to surface the top 3 pain points weekly.
- **Meeting independence:** Utilize AI meeting assistants to skip non-essential syncs and auto-generate action items directly into monday.com boards.
- **Architecture sync:** Use AI to map out dependencies between new features and existing monday.com infrastructure to avoid technical debt.

## 3. Design & prototyping (the "visual" PM)
- **AI-first wireframing:** Use generative UI tools (like Galileo or v0) to create a visual prototype for every new feature before the first designer hand-off.
- **Frictionless UX:** Identify and fix 3 major UX bottlenecks by running AI-driven heatmaps and session recording analysis.

## 4. Analysis & insights (the "data" PM)
- **Self-serve analytics:** Stop relying on Data Engineering for SQL; use AI to write queries and analyze CSV exports to identify churn or adoption patterns.
- **Predictive tracking:** Set up an automated "Product Pulse" dashboard in monday.com that alerts the team if a feature's North Star metric deviates by >10%.

## 5. Presentations & stakeholder alignment
- **AI slide deck generation:** Use Gamma or Canva Magic Design to turn a PRD into a polished stakeholder presentation in under 10 minutes.
- **Narrative crafting:** Use AI to tailor presentation voice — shifting between "Highly Technical" for engineers and "Value-Driven" for the C-suite.
- **Visual storytelling:** Use AI image generation to create custom visuals that explain complex product concepts better than bullet points.

## 6. Shipping & execution (the "delivery" PM)
- **Automated release kits:** Generate all launch collateral (release notes, Slack updates, Knowledge Base articles) automatically from the "Done" column on the board.
- **AI-assisted QA:** Use AI to generate comprehensive edge-case checklists for every feature to reduce post-launch bugs by 25%.
- **The 1-click handover:** Move a feature from "Idea" to "Dev-Ready" within monday.com using AI to fill in technical specs and sub-tasks.

---

> **Commitment:** Prioritize system building over manual execution to reclaim 10 hours of "Deep Work" per week for high-level product strategy.
