# Monetizing Agents: A New Playbook

## Slide 1: Title
**Monetizing Agents: A New Playbook**
What changes when the platform stops managing work and starts doing it

---

## Slide 2: From Managing Work to Doing Work
monday.com was where teams manage work. Now agents do the work inside the platform.

- Before: Users track and organize work that happens elsewhere
- After: Agents execute work directly — the platform is the system of action
- This changes who pays, what they pay for, and how we charge

---

## Slide 3: What Does This Mean?
Four things change at once. Each one reshapes our monetization approach.

1. **ICP changes** — the buyer shifts from IC to admin/manager
2. **Users change** — the active users become agents, not people
3. **North star changes** — from active users to tasks completed
4. **Pricing model changes** — from seat-based to consumption-based

---

## Slide 4: The ICP is Changing
The buyer shifts from IC to admin/manager. Governance becomes the product.

- The admin cares about: What did the agent do? Can I trust it? Can I control it?
- Visibility into agent work used to happen outside the platform (1:1s, Slack, updates). Now it happens inside.
- Governance and visibility aren't features — they ARE the product for this buyer

---

## Slide 5: The Users Are Changing
The platform now has two types of users: agents do the work, humans review it.

- Agents execute tasks, produce output, consume resources
- Humans shift from doing to reviewing, approving, and steering
- The user base is no longer just people — it's people + agents working together
- This breaks every assumption about DAU, engagement, and seats

---

## Slide 6: The North Star is Changing
From active users to tasks completed. This gates our CVR focus.

- Old: active users, seats, platform engagement
- New: tasks completed, value delivered, work done by agents
- If value = work completed → CVR is driven by making output visible and trusted
- No visibility into agent work = no perceived value = no conversion

---

## Slide 7: The Pricing Model is Changing
From seats to consumption. Credits align cost with value.

- Agents consume credits when they work. Admin sets the budget.
- Credit cost scales with task complexity
- Framing: "You're setting a budget for your digital workforce"

---

## Slide 8: Agent = Employee
The mental model that gates everything after this slide.

- A manager pays for an employee when they see productivity and results
- Same with agents: the admin pays when they see the agent's output and trust it
- Credits = salary. Dashboard = performance review. Governance = HR.
- This analogy drives how we convert: show value, build trust, improve quality.

---

## Slide 9: Conversion Levers
Three things drive an admin from trial to paying — all flow from the agent=employee logic.

- **Visibility** — the performance review. Dashboard showing output, time saved, ROI. No visibility = no conversion.
- **Trust & Governance** — the HR system. Permissions, escalation, audit trail. Trust separates trial from production.
- **Quality** — the training program. Better agents = more usage = more credits. Quality is a monetization lever.

---

## Slide 10: Competitive Landscape
The market is converging on consumption-based pricing.

- **Salesforce Agentforce:** $0.10/action (credits). Shifted from $2/conversation — unpredictability blocked adoption
- **ServiceNow:** Per-assist consumption (1-25+ assists/task). Sales-led, low transparency
- **Lindy AI:** Credits, self-serve. Free (400/mo) → Business ($299/mo)
- **HubSpot & ClickUp:** AI bundled into free tier. Adoption up, monetization limited.

61% of SaaS moving to usage-based or hybrid pricing by 2026

---

## Slide 11: What We Need to Build
The product roadmap behind this playbook.

- **Activity Dashboard** — performance review for agents. Visibility = conversion.
- **Governance Controls** — HR for agents. Permissions, escalation, audit trail.
- **Credits System** — payroll for digital labor. Budgets, tracking, alerts.
- **Pricing Education** — onboarding that teaches "hiring digital workers," not buying software.
- **Quality Loop** — feedback that makes agents better, justifying more spend.
