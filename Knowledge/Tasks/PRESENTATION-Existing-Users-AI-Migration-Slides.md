- **Existing customers & the new AI platform**  
- Adoption, incentives, and migration (voluntary)

---

### Slide 2 — Vision (north star)
- **One coherent AI platform story** for monday: existing users **discover**, **try**, and can **choose** the **new packaged** offer—without forced cutover.  
- **New logos** (from May 6): new plan lineup only (separate policy).  
- **Existing base:** **Surface** entitlements + **what’s new**; **incentives** where it matters (activation vs migration).  
- **Principle:** Same **commercial** destination for migration promos—**one SKU, one discount**; **messaging** and **surface** vary.

---

### Slide 3 — Why now (problem)
- Two-track risk: **legacy + add-on** vs **AI-inclusive** plans for new buyers.  
- Without action: **under-discovery** of platform, **support** load, slower **unified** adoption.  
- **Goal:** Voluntary migration with **clear TCO** and **trust**.

---

### Slide 4 — Strategy (one screen)
| Pillar | What |
|--------|------|
| **May 6** | All existing: **visibility** + **new platform CTA**; **80%+ grant use** → **extra credits** |
| **End May** | **Agents** monetization → **agents-only trial** (+ credits) |
| **Purchase** | **One migration discount** (e.g. credits/terms promo—**TBD**) at **add-on** path & **high usage**—same SKU |

### Slide 5 — Timeline
- **May 6:** Platform + comms **everywhere**; **grant 80%** cohort gets **top-up**.  
- **End May:** **Agents** trial wave (after monetization live).  
- **Ongoing:** **Migration offer** in purchase flows + high-usage surfaces.  
- **Post-promo:** Clear **transition** to standard terms (duration **TBD**—e.g. after 6 months promotional credits).

---

### Slide 6 — Deep dive: May 6 — broad existing base
- **Who:** All eligible **existing** paid workspaces (scope TBD with Legal).  
- **What:** **Both**: surface **existing** credits/entitlements **and** introduce **new** platform capabilities.  
- **CTA:** Primary → **new AI platform mode** (entry consistent across surfaces).  
- **Success:** Impressions → **click** → **first meaningful action** on platform.

---

### Slide 7 — Deep dive: May 6 — “80% grant” cohort
- **Who:** Accounts that consumed **≥80%** of the **one-time purchase grant**.  
- **What:** **Additional credits** to **experiment** on the **new platform** (bucket/validity with Finance).  
- **Why:** They’ve proven intent; reduce **hard stop** without a **better** story than “buy more add-on.”  
- **Risks:** Definition of **80%**, fairness vs light users—message as **usage-based help**, not favoritism.

---

### Slide 8 — Deep dive: End of May — Agents
- **When:** After **agents** monetization ships.  
- **What:** **Agents-only trial** + **additional credits** (separate beat from May 6 blast).  
- **Success:** Trial start → **first agent run** → attach to paid agents policy.

---

### Slide 9 — Deep dive: Purchase — migration offer (single SKU)
- **Offer:** **Discount** to adopt **new packaged offering** **instead of** buying **AI add-on** again.  
- **Example (TBD):** Promotional terms on **credits**—e.g. **no charge / allowance for 6 months**—then standard packaged terms.  
- **Same** discount terms and **SKU** for: (a) accounts **in add-on purchase flow**, (b) **high AI usage** accounts.  
- **Only** **population** and **copy** differ—no competing promo stacks.

---

### Slide 10 — Deep dive: Messaging matrix (same deal, two doors)
| Audience | Entry | Message angle |
|----------|--------|----------------|
| **Add-on intent** | AI add-ons checkout / pricing | “Before you buy more add-on—see **bundled** TCO” |
| **High usage** | Banners / admin usage surfaces | “You’re a power team—**simplify** billing with **one** packaged offer” |
| **Guardrail** | Overlap | **One** promo UI if both true |

---

### Slide 11 — Deep dive: Post-promo transition (month N+1)
- **Open:** Exact **duration** (e.g. 6 months) and **renewal** copy.  
- **Need:** No **surprise** step-change; **advance** notice in-product + email.  
- **Owner:** Finance + Legal + Lifecycle.

---

### Slide 12 — Metrics & guardrails
- Activation (platform), migration **funnel**, **support** volume, **margin** per cohort.  
- **Counter-metrics:** Churn, NPS, billing disputes.  
- **Decision:** Pause promo if guardrails breach.

---

### Slide 13 — Open decisions
- Discount **shape**: % vs **free credits for N months**; invoice line behavior.  
- **80%** grant: bucket + window.  
- **Eligibility** overlap UX.  
- **Agents** vs platform grant interaction.
