# Strategy: Incentivise existing users with free AI credits (May 6 → June 8)

**Owner:** Roni  
**Last updated:** Apr 2026  
**Inputs used:** Only the screenshots shared in chat (timeline + May 6 grant slide + June 8 agents slide)  
**Framework:** Problem → the two steps → open topics (communication + experience)

---

## Problem

Existing users are at risk of **not adopting** the new AI features fast enough because:

- Many still sit on **legacy entitlements** and don’t realize they have credits or what’s new.
- A small but important cohort hits **credit exhaustion**, creating a hard stop and a “buy more” reflex before they’ve tried the **new** experience.
- Agents is a new value surface that needs a **separate adoption beat**, otherwise it gets lost (or feels like another paywall).

**Goal:** use **targeted free-credit refills + guided UX** to push discovery and experimentation, then use a second beat to drive agents adoption.

---

## Step 1 — May 6: One-time grant (refill of credits)

May 6 is the “refill of credits” step (segmented into **No-touch / Mid-touch / High-touch**).

### Who / segmentation

The strategy splits existing users into two macro groups:

1. **3%**: credits exhausted (marked as **80% exhausted**)
2. **97%**: still have credits

Additionally, the refill amount depends on a segment labeled **“Used over 70%”** with three levels: **Low-touch / Mid-touch / High-touch**.

### What we do

**For the 3% (80% exhausted):**

- Give **more credits**.
- Show a **pop-up** that highlights the **new features and layout**.

**For the 97% (with credits):**

- **Surface** that they have credits to use.
- Show a **pop-up** that highlights the **new features and layout**.

### Refill amounts (from the May 6 slide)

| Segment (Used over 70%) | Refill of credits WM | Refill of credits CRM |
|---|---:|---:|
| Low-touch | 2000 | 3000 |
| Mid-touch | 2500 | 3500 |
| High-touch | 3000 | 4500 |

Open point in the slide: the meaning of **WM vs CRM** should be explicit in the user-facing experience (see open topics).

---

## Step 2 — June 8: Monetize agents + another refill to try agents

June 8 is the “Monetize agents” step, with an explicit incentive:

- **Another refill** to try agents

Key intent: create a **second beat** (separate from May 6) that pushes adoption of agents specifically, using credits as the on-ramp.

---

## Open topics to address (communication + experience)

### Communication

- **3% vs 97% messaging:** make it feel fair (“you’re getting a refill because…”) without fear-inducing “you’re almost out” language.
- **Segment naming:** “No-touch / Mid-touch / High-touch” is internal; define external-safe copy (or keep this logic invisible).
- **WM vs CRM explanation:** if users can observe differences, define a simple explanation; otherwise avoid surfacing the distinction.
- **Cadence:** avoid users feeling spammed across May 6 and June 8; June 8 shouldn’t read like “rules changed again.”
- **Expectation setting:** clarify whether refills are **one-time** vs a program; clarify if/when credits expire.

### Experience

- **Pop-up content + CTA:** “new features and layout” needs a concrete CTA to an immediate “aha” action (not just a tour).
- **Where credits are surfaced:** pick canonical place(s) so May 6 and June 8 feel consistent.
- **Eligibility edge cases:**
  - Users who are both “80% exhausted” and in a high-touch segment.
  - Accounts with multiple products (WM + CRM): how credits appear and how consumption works.
- **Avoid confusion:** prevent refills feeling arbitrary/buggy/inconsistent across team members.
- **June 8 agents first-run:** define what “try agents” means in one session (template → first run → value) so credits translate into usage.
