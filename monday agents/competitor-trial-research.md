## Competitive Landscape

| Company | Trial Approach | Pricing Model | Key Differentiator |
|---------|---------------|---------------|-------------------|
| **Salesforce (Agentforce)** | Free Developer Edition + Trailhead sandboxes | Per-agent licensing, bundled with platform | Guided onboarding via Trailhead — build a real agent in 20 min |
| **ServiceNow (Now Assist)** | No self-serve trial; sales-led demos | Consumption-based "assists" (25-150 per workflow) | Usage metering by workflow size |
| **HubSpot AI** | Freemium — AI baked into free tier | Free tier + paid upgrades | No separate trial, AI is just part of the product |
| **ClickUp AI** | Freemium — AI included in free plan | Free tier with limits | Low friction, AI as default feature |
| **Lindy AI** | Freemium, 60-second setup | Usage-based | First agent running in under a minute |

## Key Trends

1. **Freemium > time-limited trials** — most competitors embed AI into free tiers rather than gating behind a 14-day clock
2. **"Build something real" onboarding** — Salesforce leads with Trailhead, where you create a working agent during the trial (aha moment before paywall)
3. **Consumption-based pricing** — ServiceNow's "assists" model: pay-per-use rather than flat seat pricing
4. **Human-in-the-loop as differentiator** — platforms emphasizing user control over autonomous agents win enterprise trust

## Implications for monday.com

- **Activation risk:** If monday Agents requires lengthy setup before users see value, we lose to competitors offering 60-second agent creation. Time-to-aha-moment is critical.
- **Pricing model decision:** Consumption-based (like ServiceNow) vs. bundled freemium (like HubSpot) is a major strategic fork. Freemium drives adoption; consumption drives revenue alignment.
- **The Trailhead playbook:** Salesforce's guided "build your first agent" experience is the gold standard. Can monday offer a similar guided first-agent experience?
