## 1. May 6 — **existing accounts plan** (summary)

This section reflects the leadership recap (deck slides 1–14). It is intentionally **plain-language** and avoids naming owners.

### 1.1 Product experience for existing → rollout to new AI work platform

Starting **May 6**, existing customers gradually transition to the **new AI work platform** experience (including: **AI layout**, **Agents**, and **re-onboarding**) according to the rollout plan.

### 1.2 Monetization for existing → when credits start applying

- Starting from **May 6**, AI products start consuming **AI credits** for customers that **didn't purchase them before** (**Notetaker**, **Sidekick**, **Vibe**).
- If a customer already paid for an add-on, it **stays** on the **same pricing model**.
- **Simplification option under consideration** (feasibility pending Payments/CPQ for May 6):
  - (1) **Paid for any add-on before** → stays on old model (even when buying other AI products)
  - (2) **Didn’t pay for any add-on before** → all AI products move to **consume credits**

### 1.3 Trial credits refill for existing customers → May 6 grant

- Decision: bump **everyone** up with a **one-time grant on May 6**, with a **clear time limit**.
- This is regardless of the Agents dedicated trial.
- **Open**: final grant size (e.g. **1,000** vs **2,000** credits) + expiry.

### 1.4 Existing accounts process to switch to the new pricing model

- Phase 1: voluntary via **CX** or **sales rep** — **May 6 → May 20**
- Phase 2: voluntary via **in-product flow** — from **May 20**
- Phase 3: mandatory switch upon **renewal** — **not decided**; re-open discussion in **~1 month**
  - Option discussed: mandate the switch for **No-touch <15 seats** on **July 1**

### 1.5 Existing accounts motivation to switch to new pricing model

Approved: accounts switching to the new monetization will receive:
- Free **Vibe live apps** according to their tier
- **No-touch** accounts: **1 year discount**
- **Touch** accounts: discounted bundle + **AI Transformation** Professional Services package

Other notes:
- New AI credits add-on purchase will receive Vibe live apps according to their tier (like new users).
- Accounts that already purchased Vibe add-on: allow rep discretion to discount (touch) if there’s risk the account wants to remove their live apps; confirm discounting policy when migrating existing Vibe.

### 2.1 May 6 — help people **see** credits and try what’s new

| Who (simple) | What they get | What happens at the limit | Timing |
|--------------|---------------|---------------------------|--------|
| **Everyone eligible** (existing paid — exact eligibility with Legal) | Clear **visibility** of balances + **what’s new** on the AI platform, with **one main “try it” path** | Normal product rules still apply; this beat is about **discovery**, not changing physics | **May 6** |
| **Teams who used “most” of their one-time purchase grant** (e.g. **~80%+** — exact rule in Finance / PM) | **Extra credits** so they can **experiment** instead of hitting a wall | Same as today for hard limits; the gift **adds** runway | **May 6** |

| Who | What they experience | When limits apply | Timing |
|-----|----------------------|-------------------|--------|
| **Trial accounts** | **Unlimited** Sidekick for the trial — no daily cap, **no** charge to the team AI pool for Sidekick | **No metering** for Sidekick during trial | While trial is active (exact billing states with Finance) |
| **Free accounts (not in trial)** | **Sidekick is off** — we don’t show it as available; if someone has an old link, they see **start trial** or **upgrade** | **No access** | Once rule is live |
| **Paid accounts (after Sidekick “credits” launch)** | Each person gets a **small daily included allowance** that **resets every day** and **does not roll over**: **Basic, Standard, Pro** — **5** Sidekick units per user per day; **Enterprise** — **100** per user per day. **After that**, further Sidekick use draws from the **same shared AI credit balance** the team uses elsewhere. | **Hard stop** if daily is used up **and** the team balance can’t cover the next action — clear message for **member vs admin** | **May 6:** stop selling the **$10 Sidekick add-on**; **new Sidekick charging model** ships **after** May 6 — confirm **one** internal go-live date before customer comms |
| **Between May 6 and Sidekick go-live** | Sidekick still works under **today’s** rules; customers may see **banners** that pricing is updating | Whatever applies **today** until switch | Short transition window |
| **Older Sidekick commercial deals** (legacy SKUs) | Handled on a **separate** migration track — not the same deadline as “turn on metering” | **TBD** | Out of scope of the core Sidekick PRD |

## 6. If someone asks “is this mandatory migration?”

**Voluntary packaged migration** for the broader company story: we **surface** the new AI platform and **incentives**, but **forced SKU flip** for all legacy accounts is **not** what the migration incentives workdoc describes. **Per-feature** rules (Vibe / Sidekick / Notetaker) still have **hard stops** when someone **uses** AI without balance — that’s product behavior, not “we’re migrating your contract tonight.”
