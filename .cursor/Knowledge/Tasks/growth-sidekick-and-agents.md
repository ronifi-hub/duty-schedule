## Competitor Trial Research (Feb 25, 2026)

| Company | Trial Approach | Pricing Model | Key Differentiator |
|---------|---------------|---------------|-------------------|
| **Salesforce (Agentforce)** | Free Developer Edition + Trailhead sandboxes | Per-agent licensing, bundled with platform | Guided onboarding via Trailhead — build a real agent in 20 min |
| **ServiceNow (Now Assist)** | No self-serve trial; sales-led demos | Consumption-based "assists" (25-150 per workflow) | Usage metering by workflow size |
| **HubSpot AI** | Freemium — AI baked into free tier | Free tier + paid upgrades | No separate trial, AI is just part of the product |
| **ClickUp AI** | Freemium — AI included in free plan | Free tier with limits | Low friction, AI as default feature |
| **Lindy AI** | Freemium, 60-second setup | Usage-based | First agent running in under a minute |

**Key trends:** Freemium winning over time-limited trials. "Build something real" onboarding (Salesforce Trailhead). Consumption-based pricing emerging. Human-in-the-loop as enterprise trust differentiator.

**Implications for monday.com:**
- Time-to-aha-moment is critical — competitors offer 60-second agent creation
- Pricing fork: freemium (drives adoption) vs. consumption (drives revenue alignment)
- Salesforce's guided "build your first agent" is the gold standard to benchmark against
